2bScope — written spec
Doomless — scope
Android, portrait · dark UI · deadpan copy · one number, one line, one action
Core idea
Reads screen time and counts short-form reels seen today. A space-suited mascot is your brain: every 100 reels his tether pays out and he drifts further into deep space — Fresh (surface) → Buzzed → Dizzy → Fried → Cooked (no signal). At the daily limit he's cooked and the app shows a recovery countdown to midnight.
In scope — free
· Onboarding, 7 steps (progress + skip on 2–7)
· Screen-time permission, daily limit 120 / 240 / 400
· Home: today's count, drift depth, stage, progress to next stage, yesterday + 7-day average
· Cooked screen at the limit: countdown, no app locking
· Overlay counter while scrolling, 1 basic style, draggable, on/off
· Friends leaderboard, up to 5 · History, last 7 days
· Settings: limit, notifications, suit colour, replay onboarding
In scope — Pro (subscription)
· Lock apps at the limit (the actual lock is paid; free only warns)
· 6 extra counter styles: mascot bubble, depth gauge, oxygen bar, minimal dot, retro LCD, boss health bar
· Buy 50 more reels after lockout
· Full history beyond 7 days · more than 5 friends
· Plans: monthly $4 / yearly $19 (save 60%), 7-day trial
· Paywall as a modal from every locked row
Screens (15)
01 Splash · 02 Concept · 03 Permission · 04 Limit · 05 Friends · 06 Notifications · 07 Paywall · 08 Home · 09 Cooked/lockout · 10 Friends · 11 History · 12 Settings · 13 Paywall modal · 14 Overlay counter · 15 Counter style picker
Rules
· 4 tabs max: Home · Friends · History · Settings
· No nested navigation · hit targets ≥ 44px
· One primary action per screen · no explanatory paragraphs
· 1–2 background colours; purple is the only accent
Out of scope
· iOS · tablet · web
· Blocking individual apps one by one
· Social feed, comments, sharing
· Rewards, coins, streak shop
· Mascot animation states beyond the 5 stages
